What happens before any follow-up

01

Six-column input

Provide quote_id, sent_at, quoted_value, status, last_contact_at and owner for up to 100 sent quotes. No CRM access or integration is required.

02

Exclude safely

Remove won, lost, disputed, opted-out and stale estimates that require new pricing.

03

Prioritise

Rank the remaining estimates by value, age and observable engagement without inventing intent.

04

Approve

The client reviews every recipient, message and channel before any follow-up can happen.
